# Recordings

Delegate recordings to a Codex subagent with browser/computer use in a fresh
isolated session. Give it the app or preview URL, the input-to-outcome journey to
demonstrate, representative data, viewport and theme, and the evidence you need.
Follow the repository's capture requirements.

Ask the subagent to use a recorder supported by its available tools, capture the
real interaction and export the video. Completion means it has opened the exported
file, checked that the meaningful input and expected result are visible, and returned
the local artifact path with what the recording proves and any limitations.

Review that evidence before publishing it under its claim in the PR. If the
subagent cannot record or export, report the missing capability and provide the
strongest available evidence without claiming a recording was made.
